06 / REPRODUCIBILITY & LIMITS

What this demonstrates.

Measured

Seed variability, participant-fold transfer, publisher-pose ablation, root-centering ablation, and synthetic translation sensitivity. All candidates were fixed before new test scoring.

Still unverified

Clinical form labels, prospective intervention benefit, injury prediction, calibrated 3D accuracy, and generalization to a new institution, camera or astronaut. Source, assistance and cohort remain confounded.

Next independent check

Use clinician-reviewed intervals with explicit visibility and uncertainty labels, and a genuinely new participant/source holdout. Keep subjective form conclusions separate from the automatic measurement teacher.

Experiment record

The gait model takes a 30-frame, one-second window of 17 named 2D joints, confidence and validity masks, plus view/environment context. The random TCN has 261,387 parameters; the fine-tuned MotionBERT has 42,539,592. Outputs are 10 measurements, 13 candidate review labels and 10 evaluability scores. Quality and reference gates determine which review flags can be shown.
